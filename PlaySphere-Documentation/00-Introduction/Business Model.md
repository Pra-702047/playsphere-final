# Business Model

PlaySphere operates on a scalable B2B2C (Business-to-Business-to-Consumer) SaaS marketplace model.

## Revenue Streams

### 1. Transaction Commission (Take Rate)
- A percentage-based commission is levied on every successful online booking processed through the platform.
- This covers payment gateway fees, platform maintenance, and customer support.

### 2. SaaS Subscription (Future Roadmap)
- Tiered monthly or annual subscription plans for Turf Owners.
- **Basic Tier:** Essential booking and ledger management.
- **Premium Tier:** Advanced analytics, priority placement in search results, SMS/WhatsApp notification integrations, and multi-staff management.

### 3. Featured Listings
- Promoted slots within the player search interface, allowing owners to boost visibility for underutilized slots or off-peak hours.

## Value Proposition
- **For Owners:** Increased utilization rates, automated administrative tasks, and guaranteed upfront payments.
- **For Players:** Convenience, transparency, and a guaranteed slot upon arrival.
