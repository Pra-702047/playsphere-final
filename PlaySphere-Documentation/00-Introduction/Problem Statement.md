# Problem Statement

The recreational sports venue ecosystem suffers from significant operational inefficiencies and disjointed user experiences:

## For Facility Owners
- **Manual Ledger Management:** Reliance on physical ledgers or fragmented spreadsheets leads to double-bookings, revenue leakage, and inefficient time utilization.
- **Lack of Analytics:** Inability to track peak hours, revenue trends, and customer retention metrics.
- **Payment Inefficiencies:** Manual collection of cash or unverified P2P transfers results in reconciliation challenges and delayed settlements.
- **High No-Show Rates:** Without upfront secure payments, owners face revenue loss from unfulfilled reservations.

## For Players
- **Discovery Friction:** Difficulty in finding available local turfs without making multiple phone calls.
- **Lack of Transparency:** Opaque pricing and unavailability of real-time slot information.
- **Poor Booking Experience:** Cumbersome manual booking processes lacking instant confirmation and secure digital payments.
