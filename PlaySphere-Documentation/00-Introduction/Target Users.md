# Target Users

PlaySphere is designed for a dual-sided marketplace, catering to distinct user archetypes with specialized interfaces and capabilities.

## 1. Players (Consumers)
- **Demographic:** Sports enthusiasts, amateur athletes, corporate teams, and weekend warriors (typically aged 16-45).
- **Core Needs:** Quick discovery of nearby venues, transparent pricing, instant booking confirmation, and secure payment processing.
- **Interface:** Mobile-first Progressive Web App (PWA) optimized for speed and ease of use.

## 2. Turf Owners (Businesses)
- **Demographic:** Sports facility proprietors, local club managers, and multi-venue operators.
- **Core Needs:** Elimination of double bookings, automated payment reconciliation, business analytics, and staff management.
- **Interface:** Comprehensive desktop and tablet-optimized administrative dashboard.

## 3. System Administrators (Internal)
- **Demographic:** PlaySphere operations and support teams.
- **Core Needs:** Platform-wide oversight, user/turf verification, dispute resolution, and global financial reconciliation.
- **Interface:** Secure, internal Admin Dashboard with elevated privileges (Super Admin).
