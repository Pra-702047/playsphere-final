# Settlement

## Standard Flow
Payments collected via Razorpay are deposited into the central PlaySphere Razorpay merchant account. 

## Current Operation
Settlements to individual Turf Owners are currently managed manually outside the platform via standard bank transfers (NEFT/RTGS), calculated using the revenue metrics provided in the Admin Dashboard.

## Future Roadmap (Razorpay Route)
Implementing Razorpay Route will allow PlaySphere to automatically split transactions at the time of booking, routing the owner's share directly to their linked bank account while retaining the platform's commission in the primary account.
