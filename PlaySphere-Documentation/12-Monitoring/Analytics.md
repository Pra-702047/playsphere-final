# Analytics

## Business Analytics (Dashboard)
PlaySphere provides internal analytics directly to Turf Owners via the `/owner/dashboard` route, calculating:
- Total Revenue
- Total Bookings
- Active Turfs

## Web Analytics
> **Roadmap Recommendation:** Integrate Google Analytics (GA4) or Vercel Web Analytics to track user journeys, page views, and conversion rates (drop-off points during the booking flow).
