# Business Continuity

PlaySphere aims to ensure minimal disruption to Turf Owners' businesses during technical incidents.

- **Offline PWA Capability:** If the client loses internet connection, the PWA will serve the cached app shell, allowing owners to view their cached ledger, even if they cannot sync new online bookings.
- **Manual Fallback:** In a total platform outage, owners are instructed to revert to physical ledgers temporarily; post-outage, these can be manually entered into the system via the "Offline Booking" portal to reconcile the calendar.
