# Player Manual

Welcome to PlaySphere! Booking a turf is simple.

1. **Sign Up/Login:** Use your Google account or email to create an account.
2. **Find a Turf:** Use the homepage search bar to filter turfs by city and sport (e.g., "Mumbai", "Football").
3. **Pick a Slot:** Click on a turf to view its details. Select an available green time slot on your preferred date.
4. **Pay Securely:** Click "Book Now" and complete the payment via UPI, Card, or Netbanking.
5. **Check-In:** You will receive a 4-digit OTP. Show this OTP to the turf manager when you arrive at the venue to check in!
