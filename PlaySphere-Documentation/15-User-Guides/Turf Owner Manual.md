# Turf Owner Manual

As a Turf Owner, your dashboard is your central command center.

## 1. Viewing Bookings
Navigate to the **Bookings** tab to see a chronological list of all reservations. Use the filters to search by date or specific turf.

## 2. Checking in a Player
When a player arrives, ask for their 4-digit OTP. Enter it into the "Quick Verify OTP" field on your dashboard to mark them as arrived.

## 3. Adding an Offline Booking (Walk-in)
If a customer calls or walks in, go to the **Add Offline Booking** tab. Select the turf, date, and slot, and enter their name. This immediately blocks the slot online so nobody else can book it.

## 4. Processing Refunds
If a booking must be cancelled, find the booking in your list and change the status to **Refunded**. The money will be returned to the customer via Razorpay automatically (takes 5-7 days).
