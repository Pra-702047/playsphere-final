# Cancellation Policy

## 1. Player Cancellations
- Cancellations made more than 24 hours prior to the booked slot are eligible for a full refund (excluding gateway charges).
- Cancellations made within 12-24 hours may be subject to a 50% cancellation fee.
- Cancellations made less than 12 hours before the slot are strictly non-refundable.

## 2. Owner Cancellations
If a Turf Owner cancels a confirmed booking (due to maintenance, weather, or emergency), the Player will receive a 100% full refund automatically initiated by the platform.
