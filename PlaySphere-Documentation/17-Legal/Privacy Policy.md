# Privacy Policy

PlaySphere is committed to protecting your privacy.

## 1. Data Collection
We collect personal information including your name, email address, phone number, and booking history when you register and interact with our platform.

## 2. Data Usage
Your data is used exclusively to facilitate turf bookings, process secure payments, and communicate crucial transactional information (e.g., OTPs). 

## 3. Data Sharing
We share your name and phone number strictly with the owner of the turf you have booked to facilitate your physical check-in. We do NOT sell your data to third-party marketers.

## 4. Payment Data
PlaySphere does not collect, store, or process your credit card or bank details. All financial transactions are securely handled by our PCI-DSS compliant partner, Razorpay.
