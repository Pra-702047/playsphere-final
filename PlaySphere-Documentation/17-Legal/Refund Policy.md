# Refund Policy

## Platform Refunds
Refunds are strictly governed by the cancellation timeframe relative to the booked slot.

1. **Initiation:** Refunds are processed back to the original payment method (UPI, Card, Bank Account) via Razorpay.
2. **Processing Time:** Once initiated, refunds typically take 5 to 7 business days to reflect in the customer's account depending on the issuing bank.
3. **Platform Fees:** Convenience fees or platform commissions may be non-refundable depending on the specific transaction terms.
