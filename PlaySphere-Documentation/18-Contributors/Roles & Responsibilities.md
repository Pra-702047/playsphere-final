# Roles & Responsibilities

## Engineering Team
- Architecture design and maintenance.
- Ensuring PCI-DSS and data privacy compliance.
- Uptime monitoring and incident response.

## Product Management
- Roadmap prioritization (e.g., WhatsApp integration, Subscription tiers).
- User Experience (UX) auditing and feature definition.

## Sales & Onboarding
- Physically verifying turf facilities before granting "Active" status on the platform.
- Training Turf Owners on utilizing the Admin Dashboard and Offline Booking system.
