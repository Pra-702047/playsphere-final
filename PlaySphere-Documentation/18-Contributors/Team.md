# Team

The PlaySphere platform was conceptualized, designed, and engineered to bridge the gap in the recreational sports industry.

## Core Roles
- **Founding Team:** Responsible for business strategy, turf onboarding, and platform vision.
- **Engineering:** Responsible for the full-stack architecture (Next.js, Firebase), payment integration, and performance optimization.
- **Operations:** Responsible for customer support, dispute resolution, and managing turf owner relations.
