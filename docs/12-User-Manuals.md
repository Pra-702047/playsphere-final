# 13. User Manuals

## 13.1 Player Guide
1. **Signup/Login**: Click "Login" in the top right. Use your Email and Password to authenticate securely.
2. **Search Turf**: Use the Hero search bar on the homepage to filter by Location and Sport.
3. **Book Turf**: Click "Book Now" on a turf card. Select an available green slot. Proceed to checkout.
4. **Online Payment**: Complete the transaction using UPI, Cards, or Netbanking via Razorpay.
5. **Cancel Booking**: Navigate to your profile avatar -> "My Bookings". Click the "Cancel" button. Refunds (if eligible) are processed instantly.

## 13.2 Owner Guide
1. **Register Turf**: Once logged in as an Owner, navigate to your Dashboard -> "My Turfs" -> "Add New". Fill in the details.
2. **Pricing Configuration**: Set your Base Price (Weekday), Weekend Price, and Prime Time surcharge.
3. **Manage Slots (Offline Booking)**: Navigate to "Bookings". Select a slot and click "Block Slot". This prevents online users from booking it.
4. **Settlement & Reports**: View the "Earnings" tab to see your gross revenue and net payouts after platform commissions.

## 13.3 Admin Guide
1. **User Management**: Navigate to `/admin/users` to view, suspend, or elevate user roles (e.g., granting "owner" status).
2. **Turf Approval**: Review new turfs for accuracy before they appear on the public marketplace.
3. **Fraud Dashboard**: Navigate to `/admin/fraud` to view composite fraud scores for suspicious activities (e.g., high refund ratios).
4. **Platform Analytics**: Monitor overall platform health, total processed volume, and PlaySphere's net revenue.
